Logo Design

Project Description:

Showcase of two logotypes i've created, the goal was to reflect the brand's identity.

MissCurly is a fictional brand that offers curly hair products. The goal was a playful and accessible identity.

  • Target Audience: Teenagers who value a friendly brand.
  • Typography: Using a sans-serif typeface, mixing uppercase and lowercase letters with rounded edges to create a relaxed feel.
  • Color Palette: Different shades of pink to create a fun and a feminine aesthetic.

Ambessa Coffee, a real company specializing in high-quality Ethiopian coffee. The logo reflects the brand’s heritage and the name “Ambessa” which means “lion” in Amharic.

  • Target Audience: Coffee lovers who value authenticity and high-end quality.
  • Typography: Using bold, sharp and elegant Serif typeface to represent professionalism
  • Color Palette: Warm earthy tones inspired by coffee beans and Ethiopian culture.
